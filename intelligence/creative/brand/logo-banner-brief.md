## 1. Profil logosu — ChatGPT'ye gönder
```
Design 3 distinct logo concepts for a YouTube documentary channel called "The Industrial Casebook".
Channel idea: industrial dossier + archival evidence + engineering explanation. Tone: intelligent, investigative, technical, restrained, contemporary documentary.
Brand concept: premium industrial documentary + engineering dossier. Personality: intelligent, technical, investigative, industrial, evidence-led, cinematic, modern.
Direction: industrial, modern, strong, minimal enough to read as a YouTube avatar. Explore different routes (e.g. a strong abstract mark, a bold wordmark-derived symbol, a monogram) — but it must NOT look like a generic corporate "IC" logo.
Must work as a circular YouTube profile picture and remain legible at 98 px and 48 px: strong, simple I and C forms, few shapes, no thin hairlines, no small text.
Visual direction: heavy, condensed industrial lettering; thin engineering line details; optional single industrial silhouette (e.g. one crane) — keep it readable at avatar size; texture only on large versions, provide a flat version for the avatar.
Palette: dark charcoal, steel grey, parchment; one oxidised industrial accent (e.g. a rust-orange such as #C7542B — explore alternatives too).
Flat vector style, geometric, no gradients, no 3D, no texture, no grunge, no stamps.
Avoid: generic corporate initials logo, gears / factory / smokestack clichés, nostalgic factory-badge style, steampunk, detective or case-folder icons, police/true-crime cues (red string, crime tape, badges, magnifying glass, fingerprints), "TOP SECRET" motifs, atoms, lightning bolts, generic film reels, generic AI-documentary look.
For each concept: show it large on a square canvas, plus a preview inside a circle at 98 px and 48 px, on dark and light backgrounds. Give each concept a one-line rationale.
```

## 2. Banner — ChatGPT'ye gönder
```
Design a YouTube channel banner for "The Industrial Casebook", a documentary channel about machines, industries, decisions and evidence.
Canvas: 2560 x 1440 px. Keep ALL text and the key graphic inside the central safe area (about 1546 x 423 px, centered); the rest is background that may be cropped on some devices.
Text (exact, nothing else): "THE INDUSTRIAL CASEBOOK" as the main line in a condensed sans serif (IBM Plex Sans Condensed style), and a small line beneath: "Machines. Industries. Decisions. Evidence." in a monospace style (IBM Plex Mono style).
Background: a calm, sparse blend of three layers — a faint archival texture (paper tone), a thin engineering line drawing (generic industrial cutaway or schematic lines, not a specific real machine), and a subtle evidence-label element (a thin bracket or metadata line — not a folder, not a stamp). Low contrast, lots of negative space.
Palette: dark charcoal, steel grey, parchment; one restrained oxidised industrial accent (e.g. rust-orange such as #C7542B — explore alternatives). Heavy condensed title lettering, thin engineering lines, very light material texture only.
Do NOT: make a collage of video thumbnails, use a full dark-blue template look, use steampunk / factory-badge / detective styling, use photorealistic historical photos, add logos, add people, use red string / crime tape / stamps / grunge / fake aged paper, add any other text.
Provide 2 variations: dark-dominant and paper-dominant.
```
Not: güvenli alan değeri `[VARSAYIM]` (yaygın değer); yüklemeden önce YouTube'un resmî Yardım sayfasından güncel ölçü doğrulanır. Logo seçildikten sonra banner'da kullanılıp kullanılmayacağı Founder kararıyla.
